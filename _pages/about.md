My fascination with finance and economics stems from a fundamental curiosity about how capital flows shape industries, fuel innovation, and drive strategic decisions. Studying Business Administration and Management (BBA) at Universidad Pontificia de Comillas - ICADE provided me with a rigorous foundation in corporate strategy, but my drive has always pulled me toward the analytical and transactional core of the capital markets. I am deeply drawn to the intersection of traditional finance and quantitative problem-solving, which fuels my ambition to pursue both advanced studies in Financial Engineering and high-impact graduate roles in Investment Banking.

An international outlook has deeply defined my personal and academic journey. Growing up, I spent a transformative year living in Augusta, Georgia, during high school, which shaped my adaptability and global perspective. This international foundation later expanded during my university studies, spanning an upcoming exchange semester at the University of Bath School of Management and stepping onto the floor in London as a Global Markets Summer Analyst at UBS. Whether navigating new academic environments or managing fast-paced financial transactions across borders, I thrive in dynamic, cross-cultural settings.

Professionally, I have sought out environments where I can build, analyze, and execute. At UBS, I’ve supported capital raises and developed valuation models for complex sector pitches. Prior to that, I sharpened my operational and technical skillset at EY—automating public sector reporting workflows and building Power BI dashboards for a €2.6B project—and at Let'sInnovate, where I engineered automated startup databases and financial models for early-stage tech ventures. 

Beyond my corporate experiences, my commitment to the financial ecosystem is reflected in my leadership and entrepreneurial endeavors. As the Founder of STA, I established a research framework to publish data-driven briefs on emerging European companies. Simultaneously, as Vice President of the Comillas Private Equity & Venture Capital Club and an Investment Analyst at Testudo Wealth Management, I have spent my time assessing deal risk, analyzing public equities, and building hands-on valuation models.
